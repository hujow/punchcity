Features: 
- Title screen
- Board generation & display
	- Obstacles : 
		- Walls as tiles
		- Pits: player an enemies can fall in them and die
		- Crates: 
			- Can be pushed, they slide until hitting an obstacle (enemy or enviro). 
			- Player can bounce back when hitting an enemy against a crate, it destroys the crate
	- Bonus tiles which improve scoring formula (base score or mult)
		 

- UI display
- Player movement
	- Preview path
	- Direction switch (space)
	- Validation (v)
- Player characteristics (Health, Power)
- Pattern generation
- Pattern holding

- Enemies
	- Spawning
	- Display
	- Behavior
	- Characteristics (Health, Power, Pattern)
	- Classes
- Enemy waves
- Combat
	- Moving on a square with enemies hurts them and knocks them back
	- Damage value depends on the pattern step
	- Chain knockback: if several enemies are one behind another, they all get knocked back
	- If an enemy is pinned against a wall/obstacle, they get slam damage on top of normal damage

- Combo & Powers
	- Invincibility after a few successful hits
	- Superknockback: super attack when combo threshold is reached

- Turns: Player and enemies move one after the other, all enemies move one by one during their turn

- Scoring
	- Actions during player and enemy turn can trigger panel display, each panel grants a base value and a multiplier value. 
		all triggered panels value are added and calcultated to define the turn score

- Progression
	- Target score
	- Pattern deckbuilding with rarity:player can purchase new patterns in a 
	shop between levels. 
	Player can earn gold during level
	Player can earn interest by keeping money between rounds
- Levels
- Victory conditions
	- Kill all enemies

- Comic book panel feedback system

- Scoring is based on comic book panels displayed
